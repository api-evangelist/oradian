# Oradian

> Oradian is an AI-native core banking platform that enables financial institutions in Asia, Africa, and Europe to launch, operate, and scale modern banking services from the cloud.

Oradian helps banks, lenders, microfinance institutions, and fintechs replace legacy core systems with a cloud-native, API-first platform built around an open data model, extensible architecture, and AI-native extensibility. Customers go from requirement to live extension in days, not months.
